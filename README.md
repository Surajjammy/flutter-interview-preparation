## 🎤 Interview Answer Format

Every question follows a consistent structure:

1. **Short Interview Answer**
2. **Detailed Explanation**
3. **Example / Implementation**
4. **Real-World Scenario**
5. **Senior-Level Follow-ups**
6. **Common Mistakes**
7. **Quick Revision**

The goal is not just to memorize answers, but to understand
the concept and explain it confidently in an interview.

---

## 🚀 Goal

Build strong practical knowledge to confidently handle:

> **Concept → Why → How → Implementation → Scenario → Trade-offs**

Especially for **5–8+ years Senior Flutter Developer interviews**.

---

## ⭐ Philosophy

> Don't just learn what something is.
> Understand why it exists, when to use it, how to implement it,
> and what trade-offs it introduces.
